# Platform controls

These are the controls as of November 2026. Interfaces move; find the equivalent control by its label rather than its position.

## X

Sign in to the account in the browser. If the user runs several accounts, use the account switcher at the bottom left of x.com to select the owning account before opening the post, then switch back. Open the post URL and use the `More` (…) menu → Delete → Delete. Pinned posts show "Pinned"; promoted posts show "Promoted" or an ad label.

## Instagram

Sign in to the account in the browser. Open the post URL and use the … menu → Delete. Deleted posts sit in Your activity → Recently deleted for 30 days. Skip posts showing "Boosted", "Paid partnership", or a collab co-author, and skip any post with a running boost in Meta Business Suite.

## TikTok

Sign in to the account in the browser. Open the video, use the … menu → Privacy settings → "Who can watch this video" → Only me to hide it. Delete is in the same … menu. Skip videos marked Pinned or promoted (Promote / ad labels).

## YouTube

Use YouTube Studio for the channel → Content → Shorts. Hover the Short → Visibility → Private → Save to hide it. Permanent deletion is in the row's ⋮ Options → Delete forever. Ignore scheduled and draft Shorts; Survivor only ranks published ones.
