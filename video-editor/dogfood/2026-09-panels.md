# Dogfood: Panels launch film (2026-09)

First independent run of the skill on a real product: a 25 s launch film for [`@tjcages/panels`](https://github.com/tjcages/panels), a floating React dev panel. Source: [tjcages/panels#18](https://github.com/tjcages/panels/pull/18) (`launch-film/`). Request was just "create a launch video for this package using the skill."

**Verdict from the owner:** it works for the purpose, but it isn't the best video. The music was on point. The UI looked good, but the film didn't show enough of the product's breadth. Cuts to near-identical frames felt awkward. It should be snappier, with better animation and transitions.

## What worked

| What | Why it worked | Kept as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording the real runtime in slow motion | Scaling every clock the panel reads (rAF, timers, `Event.timeStamp`, CSS/WAAPI through CDP) and replaying input 30x slower gave shipped easing and throw physics at 3x resolution. No lookalike components. | `assets/capture/record.mjs` |
| Pointer from the recorded input path | Recorded pointer data, drawn with the skill's glyphs, is sharp at any zoom and matches the real interaction frame for frame | `assets/launch/footage.tsx` |
| Synthesized music bed, groove dropping on the first reveal | Original, license-free, and landed well | `assets/editor/bed.mjs` |
| `validateEdit` motion checks | Caught a direction reversal and two cuts on stills before render | unchanged |
| Filming exposed product bugs | Float panel opens top-left when mounted collapsed; buttons need a host CSS reset | fidelity.md, "Product bugs found while filming" |
| Self-contained QC loop (stills, contact sheets, frame pairs) | Caught a framing clip on the color shot and a wrong-position reveal | render.md checklist |

## What didn't

### 1. Cuts to near-identical frames (the headline problem)
Six of nine cuts went from the panel to the same panel on the same page, reframed by 0.6x to 2.0x. Each still looked fine, and the edit read as stuttering. Two checks should have caught it and neither did:
- The variety check compares self-declared `subject` names. The same panel was called "sliders", "field", "color", and "ribbons", so every cut passed.
- `qc.mjs` compared raw pixels. A small reframe of a busy picture moves every pixel, so the check scored the frames as "different".

**Fix:** `similarity.mjs` aligns the two frames (it searches zoom and offset) before scoring them on structure and colour. Measured on the real cuts, the six same-surface cuts scored 0.93-1.00 and the title cuts scored 0.58 or lower, or were far apart in colour. It now flags all six and nothing else. `Scene.surface` plus the `punch` and `handoff` continuities put the rule into `validateEdit`, and scenes.md states it plainly: a same-surface reframe is a camera move, never a cut.

### 2. Not enough breadth
The film showed 5 of roughly 15 capabilities. Shader adapters, overlay pins, export, gradient/stripe editors, presets, JSON tools, and theming were all omitted. The skill's guidance ("a coherent few distinct features") pushed toward depth with no counterweight.
**Fix:** story.md now has a default coverage contract: hook, first contact, core loop, 3-4 heroes, a **breadth montage** of 8-14 frame real glimpses, differentiator, and CTA. `montage` scenes may run 8 frames, and the inventory has to mark every capability hero, montage, or omit.

### 3. Not snappy
Takes were scripted with ~700 ms pointer approaches and ~1.5 s result holds, and clips ran 56-92 frames. The waves were always moving, so dead air didn't look static, but it still read as slow.
**Fix:** scenes.md now has pacing defaults (approach 10-12 frames, press 2-4 after arrival, hold 12-20, launch clips 30-60 frames). `shots.example.mjs` uses snappy timings.

### 4. Transitions were only cuts and drifts
The scenes guide discouraged designed transitions but offered no alternative. Scenes are concatenated with no overlap, so a cross-scene effect wasn't even possible.
**Fix:** `transitions.ts` (`flood`, `wipe`, `pushThrough`) plus the `handoff` continuity: the outgoing scene ends on a shared frame and the incoming scene starts from it. Transitions must come out of the product (the theme flood from the clicked toggle, the wipe in the drag direction).

### 5. Continuity slips between separate takes
Each shot was a separate recording that reloaded the page from saved state. UI-only state (an expanded collection row) was lost, so the row visibly collapsed across the collection→float cut.
**Fix:** one take per surface (staging.md), which also removes the same-surface cuts from #1.

### 6. Sound was measured wrong, then buried
With the editor's music volume at 0.85, every cue sat 15-40 dB under the bed, because the Cuelume samples are UI-quiet. After a manual fix to 0.3, spot checks looked fine, but the headless level report later showed the small clicks only 0-4 dB over the music in the delivered film.
**Fix:** `render-mix.mjs` builds the recipe, reports every cue's level against the bed, and masters to -14 LUFS. The default music volume is now 0.22 (16 of 17 cues clear +4 dB).

### 7. Environment friction ate time
FFmpeg was missing, Remotion's Chrome download was blocked (403), Google Fonts failed TLS inside the render browser, the 4K footage starved Remotion's 30 s font timeout, and the headless screencast was capped at CSS pixels.
**Fix:** `scripts/preflight.mjs` finds all of these in one pass. `remotion.config.ts` reads `REMOTION_CHROME` and raises the delayRender timeout. render.md says to self-host fonts. The recorder uses full-resolution screenshots, not screencast.

### 8. Small mechanics
- Scripted clicks were instant down/up, so the cursor press never showed. `click()` now holds 70 ms, and `Footage` holds the press for at least 3 frames.
- The soft cursor can't grab. The film needs one cursor family, and drags need `grab`/`grabbing`, so the recorder defaults to the native set.
- The reading-hold check was a separate call that was easy to forget. It is now `Scene.text`, checked by `validateEdit`.

## Not addressed yet (next iteration)

- **A montage builder:** a helper that turns a list of short recorded takes into a beat-aligned montage (cut points snapped to the bed's beat grid).
- **Beat-aware cutting in general:** `bed.mjs` knows its BPM and drop, and the edit could snap cut points to beats.
- **Tier drift:** footage scenes used off-tier zooms (1.12, 1.16, `PUSH * 0.93`). `validateEdit` checks declared tiers, not camera state.
- **Listening:** an agent still can't hear the mix. The level report catches buried cues, not tone or taste.
- **Linear:** no Linear access in the session. The OFF-725 dogfood issue still needs this log linked.
