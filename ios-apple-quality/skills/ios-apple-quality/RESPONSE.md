2. State that you loaded **CRAFT_SPEC** + **VISUAL_GATE** (and IPAD_CRAFT if regular width).
3. Cite **R#** rules applied (dock R57/R103, rows R104, density R105, etc.) — one line cluster is fine.

5. For design outputs: confirm **screenshot self-QA** passed R101 (hard-stop trio green).
6. Call out a11y and `#available` Liquid Glass fallbacks explicitly.
7. Prefer diffs and screenshots over prose. Keep status short. Never present a frame that fails R110.
