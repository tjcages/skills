# WIP frames chat

1. **Lead with the PNG.** Attach the board. Do not describe the board instead of showing it.
2. **One line of titles** under the image, in board order. Example: `Today · Pipeline · Deal detail`.
3. **No recap of the skill.** The image is the update.
4. **If compose fails,** say the cause and the one fix (wrong mix, missing screen, bad theme key). Do not attach a raw capture as a fallback.
5. **Theme mention only when one was used:** `theme: totem` or the hex. Skip when defaults ran.

First thing they see is the board. Last thing is a title line or a blocker.
