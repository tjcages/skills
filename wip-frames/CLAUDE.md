# WIP frames — agent instructions

## Product rules

- `shared/METHODOLOGY.md` is the source of truth.
- Keep `skills/wip-frames/SKILL.md` under about 100 lines.
- Do not bake a product, channel, or person into the skill.
- Official Figma iOS kit status bar only. Never redraw time, cellular, wifi, or battery.
- Theme may tint the mat. Theme may not add fonts, bezels, ribbons, or custom status icons.
- Allowed theme keys: mat, dot, title, title_size, pad, gap, status_opacity, pill, desktop_radius.
- Titles: SF Pro / SF NS if on disk, else Arial, else Liberation Sans. Never Inter.
- Desktop scrollbars are Apple overlay thumbs. No OS gutters.
- Attach the PNG. Do not describe the board instead of showing it.
